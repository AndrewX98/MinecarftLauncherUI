Welcome to our experimental mods section.

Managing mods is not yet supported. To contribute your mod, please open a pull request on minecraft-linux/mcpelauncher-moddb.

Mods are a collection of .so files placed inside the mods folder within your data root (see Settings › Storage). The mods folder does not exist by default. Extract zip files directly into it without creating subfolders.

Do not report crashes to the launcher’s issue tracker when mods are enabled.
